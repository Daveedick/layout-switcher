# Third-party code

The keyboard tracking, retyping and macOS Services integration are adapted from
[ShiftSwitch](https://github.com/imakarov/shiftswitch), revision
`69099f0601ee66ca620a75fe216c6a8d2fe49452`, by Ilya Makarov.

ShiftSwitch is licensed under the MIT License. The complete notice is preserved
in `Resources/Licenses/ShiftSwitch-LICENSE.txt` and copied into the built app.

Layout Switcher retains its own Option+Command gesture, mixed English/Russian
conversion, app identity, lifecycle and permission handling. It is not an
official ShiftSwitch or Punto Switcher release.
